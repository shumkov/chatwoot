# Weekly conversation-quality report

The implementation and canonical specification live in Shumabit:
[Weekly conversation-quality specification](https://github.com/shumkov/shumabit-claude/blob/main/docs/UMI-WEEKLY-CONVERSATION-QUALITY-SPEC.md).

Chatwoot supplies structured full-history and operational facts. Shumabit assesses
the reporting week against the six-rule rubric and formats the Telegram summary
and PDF. This does not authorize customer replies or turn old audit suggestions
into live reminders.

Production acceptance and historical-audit delivery are recorded in
[the historical audit specification](UMI-HISTORICAL-CRM-AUDIT-SPEC.md).
The operator-facing instructions are in [Mai's guide](UMI-MAI-CHAT-GUIDE.md).
