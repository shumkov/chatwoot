# PromptPay option for the same Shopify-linked purchase

Status: research/spec addendum for independent review, 2026-10-19. Supplements the social-commerce plan's U5/U6; does not replace its staff admission, identity, market, attribution, channel-window or duplicate-operation rules. No payment, order or message was created during research.

## Problem and decision

Mai closes Thai sales in chat with PromptPay QR as well as Shopify checkout. The proposed basket/draft must support both ways to pay without creating a second purchase or mistaking a QR, screenshot or customer claim for received funds.

Keep one linked Shopify OPEN draft as the purchase record. Offer its existing checkout link or an amount-prefilled UMI QR generated by Shumabit's existing `promptpay-qr` skill. QR generation and staff-authorized delivery are payment instructions only. After verifying money received, Mai records the manual payment against that same draft in Shopify Admin; Shopify creates its order and existing draft-link reconciliation follows the draft's order ID. The bridge does not complete drafts, mark paid, create transactions or infer receipt from images. Existing financial reconciliation remains authoritative for downstream paid state. No new bank integration, permission system, daemon or settlement classifier.

## Operator-visible flow

1. Mai agrees exact items/options/quantities and delivery/pickup terms. Existing draft preview/create produces the same linked OPEN draft, Shopify Admin URL and checkout URL privately.
2. Mai first confirms the customer-selected payment path; do not send checkout and QR unsolicited together. Checkout remains available as an alternative. For Thai QR, propose a private staff command `/payment-qr draft:<numeric-id>` (or `order:<numeric-id>` for an already-linked unpaid order). IDs are explicit object types; no free amount argument. The command reuses U1 real-staff/private-note admission and binding, with one source claim. It reads the linked current record and prepares the amount from its verified THB amount due.
3. OPEN draft QR requires a fully agreed final payable total: items, discounts, shipping, tax and relevant terms resolved. A subtotal or a total that checkout may still change is insufficient. Mai finalizes those details in Shopify Admin first; issuing the staff QR command asserts she has done so. The handler displays the current total and shipping/tax components from a fresh read, holding if required fields are unavailable. It does not infer a synthetic `final=true` state from draft fields. Existing order QR uses a new fresh GraphQL read of `totalOutstandingSet.presentmentMoney`, not the existing CommerceReader amount: that reader currently exposes original `Order.totalPriceSet`, which is unsuitable for amount due. Require outstanding currency THB and use its exact decimal amount. Reject non-THB, zero/negative, unsupported partial-payment/deposit terms, completed/stale/conflicting records or identity mismatch. Handle money as exact decimal/minor units and reject values with more than two decimal places before invoking the generator; do not let its Number/toFixed rounding decide business amounts.
4. Return a private preview: purchase ID, final THB amount, recipient UMI STORE CO., LTD./Kasikorn Bank, QR image, and “Payment instructions prepared; unpaid.” Include a copyable customer caption: “UMI purchase <draft/order ID> — ฿<exact amount> | Kasikorn Bank | UMI STORE CO., LTD. | Scan to pay (amount pre-filled). Please pay once using this chosen method.” Staff explicitly sends it using the ordinary Chatwoot attachment flow. This simplest release needs no new public-send command: reuse existing media upload/message behavior and eligible channel windows. If later automating public QR sends, use the same U1 staff source/normal row/provider ambiguity contract as product sends; do not send on a suggestion or preparation alone.
5. Customer scans and pays; a slip may help Mai investigate, but is not machine-verifiable received-funds evidence. Mai checks the receiving bank record and associates the receipt to this purchase, including amount/currency/recipient/time and unique bank transaction where available. Equal amount alone cannot associate receipts or distinguish reuse. Unmatched, partial or disputed receipts stay unpaid/held for Mai.
6. Mai opens the SAME draft in Shopify Admin, records received manual payment and creates its order, or records payment on the existing unpaid order. Shopify can issue its own confirmation email during this manual operation; the bridge does not send invoice email or confirmation. After authoritative readback, existing reconciliation updates the link and paid state. Never create another order just to represent QR payment.
7. Where appropriate Mai uses existing `/paid-in-chat #<order-number>` only after the actual order is linked and Shopify payment verifies. It records purchase source for downstream eligibility; its private result is neither a bank receipt nor a Meta delivery receipt. For an order Shopify identifies as website checkout, preserve the existing website exclusion rather than relabeling it because a QR was shared.

The private operator result identifies checkout and QR as alternatives for one purchase. Publicly send the customer-selected path, with the warning “Pay once using your chosen method.” Keep an OPEN draft until actual payment instead of automatically converting to a pending order. The generator has no expiry field or revocation path; a QR already sent remains scannable: changed terms or switching payment routes requires Mai to tell the buyer which instructions are current and check for an earlier transfer before requesting more money. Do not promise that withdrawing a message invalidates the QR.

## Alternatives and failure modes

- Pending order before QR: Shopify supports unpaid orders/payment terms, but completion changes inventory/order lifecycle and is outside U5. Not needed for an initial QR option. Use an existing pending order if already linked; do not automate draft completion solely to obtain an order number.
- Automatic bank settlement: rejected without an existing authoritative confirmation source. A future integration would need unique transaction matching, duplicate/partial/refund handling and explicit scope; current amount-only QR cannot supply that contract. No new automatic settlement is introduced by this addendum.
- Shopify-hosted checkout QR/provider: useful where the actual checkout offers it, but current method/eligibility was not verified. Keep the checkout option without claiming it offers PromptPay or completes the direct-bank path automatically.
- Generation failure: private failed status, no public send, no paid state. Changed amount after preparation: invalidate our old proposal only (the external QR remains scannable); re-read and prepare another QR only on staff request, with visible purchase ID and corrected amount in the caption. If funds already arrived, Mai reconciles instead of asking to pay again.
- Duplicate preparation may return its recorded private artifact; it cannot authorize another public message or payment write. Ambiguous public media delivery follows existing provider hold/readback behavior, without blind retry.
- Concurrent checkout/direct QR payment is possible; no claim of automatic mutual exclusion. Mai checks Shopify and bank before recording a receipt and escalates double payment for the existing refund/accounting workflow.

## Verification and implementation scope

Implement narrowly in Shumabit bridge/agent/skill documentation plus focused tests, reusing current command source claims, Shopify readers and Chatwoot media flow. No Chatwoot financial-state/schema changes are required by the QR preparation slice. Keep provenance of draft/order ID, current amount/currency, staff source and generated artifact in the private result; never encode those IDs into bank payload refs. Follow overlay/patch registry rules only if Chatwoot changes actually become necessary.

Tests must prove amount comes from the linked purchase, including an existing order whose original total differs from outstanding balance and asserting the QR uses fresh `totalOutstandingSet.presentmentMoney`; guessed amount, currency conversion, subtotal-only, duplicate amount/receipt, partial terms, stale identity, edited command and changed record cannot silently succeed. Confirm generator payload/tag/CRC matches canonical builder and a known amount fixture, including two-decimal input; do not rebuild the builder in production. QR preparation and customer slip must yield zero Shopify payment/completion mutations and no paid/conversion event. Existing draft→order link and `/paid-in-chat` reject-unpaid/website rules must still pass. A bank transfer canary is not part of implementation tests; historical skill evidence does not establish present payer-app behavior. Verify ordinary attachment preparation/display with an owner-only conversation before enabling customer use, without transferring money.

## Remaining facts to verify before activation

- Whether current live draft read fields and configured shipping/tax terms identify a genuinely final THB amount; unresolved final total blocks that QR only.
- Actual Mai receiving-bank verification practice and whether a bank/merchant integration already exists outside inspected source. Until authoritative evidence is demonstrated, Mai records payment in Shopify.
- Ordinary Chatwoot private attachment visibility/download/send behavior for Mai on each activated channel; no new UI is required.
- Real Shopify source metadata and successful manual-payment transaction shape after draft conversion. Reuse existing financial classifier rather than bypassing it if its paid evidence is insufficient.
