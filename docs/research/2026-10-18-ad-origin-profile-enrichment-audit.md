# Ad-origin profile enrichment: bounded production read

Read-only snapshot, 18 October 2026, 16:03 UTC. Chatwoot account 1, unified FB/IG inbox 2, 21-day message window from 27 September 16:03 UTC. This cohort contains messages with a **stored explicit ADS referral**, not every chat influenced by an ad.

The query scanned 1,696 inbox messages and found 12 inbound ADS-referral messages in 11 conversations/11 contacts. Five conversations were Instagram DMs (six referral messages), and six were Messenger. The placement/creative is not the message destination: the six Messenger conversations include an ad with an Instagram creative permalink. All five Instagram contacts had a non-placeholder name, avatar attachment, Instagram handle/social profile link, a profile-check timestamp, and follower/audience fields in contact attributes. The raw follower counts ranged from 184 to 12,628; audience bands, verification and follows-business flags were projected. Business bio/website was available for only one of five and should be treated as optional source data, not an enrichment failure. Two newest Messenger contacts lacked avatar and profile-check timestamp. None of the 11 had a verified Shopify customer link, email or phone in Chatwoot; social profile evidence does not itself identify a Shopify buyer.

The UI source projects contact name/avatar, Instagram social link and expandable custom attributes; this audit did **not** verify the rendered browser panel. The five Instagram conversations are Chatwoot IDs 1126, 59, 1128, 1129 and 1143; Messenger IDs 1159, 1160, 1161, 1168, 1166 and 1169. Access requires the owner's Chatwoot account. No profile retry or account mutation was made.

Practical conclusion: the observed Instagram subset is complete for the fields currently available and configured, while optional bio/website and Shopify identity need separate evidence. The two Messenger avatar gaps should be checked on their own platform path, not counted as Instagram enrichment failures. This sample cannot establish coverage for ad conversations whose referral was missing, expired or not stored.
